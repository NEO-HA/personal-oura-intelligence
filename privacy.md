Privacy Policy

Personal Oura Intelligence

Last Updated: July 2026

Personal Oura Intelligence is a private health analytics application created for personal use.

Information Collected

This application may collect, with the user's explicit authorization:

Oura Ring health data
Sleep metrics
Activity metrics
Readiness metrics
Heart rate
HRV
Blood oxygen measurements
Stress measurements
Tags
Workouts
Sessions
Other data made available through the Oura API.

The application may also store manually entered information including:

medications
alcohol consumption
cannabis use
menopause symptoms
illness
emotional events
meals
travel
exercise
personal notes
How Information Is Used

The information is used solely to provide personalized health analysis and identify patterns that cannot be inferred from wearable sensor data alone.

No automated medical diagnosis is performed.

Data Sharing

Personal Oura Intelligence does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Storage

Data is stored locally or on systems controlled by the application owner.

Security

Reasonable security measures are used including encrypted credential storage and secure authentication.

User Control

Users may revoke Oura access at any time through their Oura account settings.

Users may request deletion of locally stored information.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the application owner.
